# A Taste of Hegel — plan

## Aim
A short demo of codie: give a newcomer a feel for why Hegel is difficult and what his method is for.

## Learning objectives
- O1: Explain two reasons Hegel's prose resists a first reading.
- O2: Explain why "thesis–antithesis–synthesis" is a misleading summary of Hegel's dialectic.

## Syllabus
### 01-why-hegel-is-hard — Why Hegel Is Hard to Read
Why: sets expectations so difficulty reads as a feature of the project, not a failure of the reader. Objectives: O1.
### 02-dialectic — What "Dialectic" Does and Doesn't Mean
Why: replaces the textbook triad with a better picture. Objectives: O2. Prerequisites: chapter 1.
